# Gatherwise

## v1.2.1

- Mining a vein now clears its "live" marker, so the route no longer sends you back to a vein you just mined.
- Spotted ("live") nodes are shown in teal, never yellow, so they can't be mistaken for Blizzard's tracking dots.
- Hovering a spot that was marked live, with no yellow dot under it, clears it.
- Hovers only match a known spawn point within 15 yards; guesses from the cursor are no longer saved permanently.

## v1.2.0

- The arrow goes to a much closer stop first instead of strictly following the loop.
- Routes take height into account and avoid climbing up and down hills.

## v1.1.x

- Spawn points show as faint rings while Find Minerals / Find Herbs is on.
- Hover a yellow dot on the minimap to send the route there; hover an empty target ring (or press Alt+S) to skip it.
- Optional close-range detection with soft-targeting.

## v1.0.0

- First release: node map, skill filters, routes and guide arrow.
